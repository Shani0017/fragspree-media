Privacy Policy - FragSpree Uploader

FragSpree Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the FragSpree and Brain Massage by CN social media accounts to schedule posts and to read and reply to comments on those accounts.

What we access

Only data of the accounts that connect the app themselves: their posts, post statistics, followers count and the comments on their posts (comment text, commenter's public username and time).

How it is used

Comments are shown to the account owner so they can reply. Statistics are shown in a private dashboard. We do not sell, share or publish this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage

Data is kept on a private server operated by the account owner and is kept for as long as needed to run the dashboard.

Deletion and contact

To have any data removed, email shani.jr24@gmail.com. See also our data deletion instructions.
